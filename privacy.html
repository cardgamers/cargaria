PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE: 1 NOVEMBER 2026 OPERATOR: SHARABHA INC (USA) ZERO DATA SELLING
01 // WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect only what is necessary to provide and improve the Cargaria service:

  • [→]Account data: Your name, email address, and company name when you register.
  • [→]Business trade data: Contracts, shipment records, payment logs, and documents you enter into Cargaria.
  • [→]Usage analytics: Anonymised page-view and feature-use data via Google Analytics 4 (GA4). No personal identifiers are sent to Google.
  • [→]Technical metadata: Browser type, device type, IP address, and session identifiers for security and debugging.
02 // HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • [✓]Provide and operate the Cargaria platform for your account.
  • [✓]Send transactional emails (account confirmation, payment receipts, password reset).
  • [✓]Improve the product based on anonymised, aggregate usage patterns.
  • [✓]Respond to support requests you initiate.
  • [✓]Comply with applicable law when legally required.
03 // OUR CORE COMMITMENT

We do not sell your data. We do not use your business trade data to train machine learning models. Ever.

Your contracts, shipment records, counterparty names, and payment data belong to you. They exist in Cargaria solely to serve your trading operations — not to inform any third-party system, advertising platform, or AI training pipeline.

04 // DATA STORAGE & THIRD PARTIES

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (AWS, US region), protected by row-level security and encrypted at rest and in transit.

We share data only with the following sub-processors, strictly for service delivery:

SUPABASEDatabase & Auth infrastructure (AWS US)
GOOGLE ANALYTICSAnonymised usage analytics only
CLOUDFLARECDN, DNS & DDoS protection

No other third party has access to your account or business data.

05 // YOUR RIGHTS & DATA RETENTION

You have full control of your data. You may at any time:

  • [→]Access: Export all your account and trade data in CSV format from within the app.
  • [→]Correct: Edit any data directly in Cargaria.
  • [→]Delete: Request full account and data deletion within 30 days by emailing privacy@cargaria.com.
  • [→]Port: Download your data in machine-readable format at any time.

We retain account data for as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after account closure for backup and dispute resolution purposes, unless you request earlier deletion.

06 // COOKIES

Cargaria uses a minimal set of cookies:

  • [→]Session cookie: Required for authentication. Deleted when you sign out.
  • [→]GA4 analytics cookie: Anonymised usage tracking. No cross-site tracking or advertising profiling.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

07 // CONTACT & CHANGES

For any privacy questions or data requests, contact us at: privacy@cargaria.com

We will notify users by email of any material changes to this policy at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of Cargaria after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
