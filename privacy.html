This Privacy Policy describes how Cargaria ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our trade management platform at cargaria.com.

1. What we collect

We collect only what's needed to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and company name when you register.
  • Trade data — the trades, shipments, documents, counterparties, and payments you enter into Cargaria. This is your operational data and belongs to you.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and time spent in the app. We use this in aggregate to improve the product.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system, collected automatically when you access the service.

2. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Cargaria platform.
  • Send transactional emails — account creation, password resets, and service notices.
  • Understand how the product is used so we can build better features.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your trade data for any purpose other than running your workspace. We do not sell it. We do not mine it for insights to sell to third parties.

3. Storage & security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices including HTTPS for all data transmission.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. We work hard to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security. If you become aware of any security issue, please contact us immediately at hello@cargaria.com.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or trade data with third parties except:

  • Service providers — infrastructure and hosting providers (such as Supabase and Cloudflare) who process data on our behalf under strict agreements.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger or acquisition, with advance notice to you.

5. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Session cookies — to keep you logged in while you use the app.
  • Analytics cookies — via Google Analytics 4, to understand aggregate usage patterns. These do not contain your trade data.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the service may not work correctly without them.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Export your trade data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@cargaria.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Cargaria is a business tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you by email. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data? Write to us at hello@cargaria.com. We're a small team and we respond to every message.
