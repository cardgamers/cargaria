About Cargaria

Built by traders who got tired of managing deals across spreadsheets, email threads, and notebooks.

Why we built this

Commodity trading runs on relationships and information. But most independent traders and small brokerages still manage their entire trade book in spreadsheets — tracking shipments in one tab, documents in another, payments in a third, and counterparty notes somewhere in an email chain they can no longer find.

When a vessel is delayed, a document goes missing, or a buyer asks for a status update, the answer is buried. That's time wasted — and in a margin-thin business, time is money.

Cargaria is built to fix that. One place for the whole trade lifecycle: from the first enquiry to the final payment. Clean, fast, and built for the way physical traders actually work.

Who it's for

Cargaria is built for independent commodity traders, small brokerages, and trading desks that handle physical goods — timber, agri-commodities, minerals, metals, industrial inputs, and more. If you move physical product across borders and need to track it properly, Cargaria is for you.

We're not trying to serve Fortune 500 trading floors. Those teams have enterprise platforms. We're here for the trader who works alone or with a small team, closes real deals, and deserves a proper tool — not a subscription to something built for someone else.
