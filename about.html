[01] THE ORIGIN STORY

Why We Built This

Physical commodity trading is operationally complex. You're coordinating suppliers, forwarders, shipping lines, buyers, and banks across multiple time zones — managing contracts worth hundreds of thousands of dollars with nothing but a chain of emails and a shared spreadsheet.

When a container goes into detention, you typically find out when the invoice arrives. When a TT advance is overdue, you're chasing WhatsApp messages across time zones. When your accountant asks for the quarter's P&L, you're stitching together data from three different tools.

Cargaria exists because we lived that problem for years and decided to solve it properly.

[02] THE PROBLEM

Every Feature Exists Because We Needed It.

PAIN #01

Detention Blindness

Free days tracked in a PDF. Arrival dates buried in a forwarder's email. The only alert was a $6,000 invoice arriving 2 weeks after containers breached free time.

PAIN #02

Document Chaos

"Which version of the BL is the final one?" "Who has the original Phytosanitary cert?" Documents scattered across email threads, WhatsApp, and shared drives with no single source of truth.

PAIN #03

Phantom P&L

Quarter-end reconciliation meant manually re-tracing every cost: freight adjustments, survey fees, detention charges not yet invoiced. Real margins only visible weeks after the deal closed.
